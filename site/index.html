Privacy, stated plainly

What stays on your phone, and what crosses the network

This table was read out of the source code, not written from marketing intent. If something sends data, it's listed.

Stays on your device

  • Prayer times — computed locally by the adhan package from your coordinates.
  • Qibla bearing — a great-circle calculation plus your sensors, no lookup.
  • The whole Qur'an text — shipped inside the app as a bundled Uthmani file.
  • Nasheeds — every playable track is bundled in the app's Anashed library, so nothing streams.
  • Your location — read on-device for prayer times, Qibla and notifications; never sent anywhere.
  • Your progress — adhkar counts, goals, streaks, badges, notes and bookmarks in local storage.

Crosses the network

  • Recitation audio — surah files streamed or saved from cdn.mp3quran.net and cdn.islamic.network; the reciter list is fetched from mp3quran.net.
  • Extra Hadith collections — the verified seed ships inside the app; optional collections come from cdn.jsdelivr.net (the open fawazahmed0 hadith API) and are then cached on your phone.
  • Quran Race rooms — live room state through Firebase Realtime Database, under an anonymous ID the app creates on launch. It is the only identifier the app holds, and it carries no name, email or phone number.

Absent by construction

  • Ads
  • Analytics SDK
  • Crash reporting
  • Account
  • Email
  • Password
  • Phone number

No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK exists in the build, and there is nothing to sign up with. Because there is no server of its own, the app cannot read your prayer log, your notes or your location — those live on your phone and leave it only if you use the built-in data export yourself.

Questions

Before you install it

Why an APK instead of the Google Play Store?

The app is distributed straight from its own repository for now, so you download AlNoor from GitHub Releases and update it the same way. Nothing stands between you and a new build, and there is no store account attached to your usage.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no in-app purchase, and no ads funding it. Every feature on this page is included in the download, and the themes and badges you can unlock cost experience earned by praying — never money.

What works offline?

Prayer times, the entire Qur'an text, Qibla direction, adhkar, tasbeeh, the Hijri calendar, the nasheed library, your saved items and your progress all work with no connection at all. Recitation audio needs a connection the first time, and you can save a surah for offline listening. The bundled hadith seed is offline too — only the extra CDN collections need a connection.

Which Android version do I need?

Android 7.0 (Nougat, API 24) or newer. Phone and tablet layouts both work.

How do I get updates?

New builds land on the same Releases page. Installing a newer APK over the old one keeps your progress, because everything is stored on the device rather than on a server.

Is my location stored or uploaded?

No. Your coordinates are read on the device, fed into the local prayer-time and Qibla calculations, and used to schedule notifications. Nothing location-related is sent to any server. If you'd rather not grant permission at all, you can set your coordinates manually on the Qibla screen.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The codebase is Flutter and an iOS project exists, but there is no published App Store or TestFlight build today, so AlNoor is Android-only for now.
